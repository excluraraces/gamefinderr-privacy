Your preferences

Quiz answers are processed on your device to produce game recommendations and your player profile. The app does not require an account or send quiz answers to the developer's server.

Information stored on your device

Favorite games, language and sound settings are stored in the app's local storage. This version does not use advertising or in-app analytics services.

Retention and deletion

You can remove favorites in the app. To delete all local records, clear the app's data in Android settings. Depending on your system settings, your device's backup service may back up or restore app data.

Support

The support link opens your email app. Messages you send and your email address are processed through the email provider to respond to your request. You can request deletion of your support correspondence at the same address. Do not include sensitive information in your message.
